Platform features
Everything you need to understand local climate risk
🗺️
25-Cell Micro-Zone Grid
Each scan generates a 5×5 grid of cells centred on your location. Every cell is independently scored and colour-coded — giving you a hyper-local picture of risk, not just a city-level average.
🌤️
Real-Time Climate Data
Live data from the OpenWeatherMap API: current temperature, cloud cover percentage, AQI proxy, estimated elevation, and urban density — refreshed on every scan.
🤖
Self-Learning AI Model
After each scan you can rate the accuracy. The AI adjusts its internal weights — up when correct, down when wrong — so the model gets more accurate with every piece of feedback.
📊
History & Leaderboard
All searches are saved and accessible via the History tab. The Leaderboard shows the top 10 highest-risk zones ever scanned globally — sorted by AI risk score.
📍
Multiple Input Methods
Search by Indian pincode, full address, or let the browser use your GPS coordinates. Any of the three routes you into the same rich risk dashboard instantly.
🌙
Light & Dark Mode
Switch between a deep dark geo-intelligence theme and a clean light mode at any time using the toggle in the top bar. Your preference is saved for next time.
What we measure
5 climate signals,
one unified score
ZoneRisk combines five independent climate signals into one weighted risk score per zone. The AI model adjusts the weight of each signal over time based on your feedback, making the score progressively more accurate for your region and area type.
